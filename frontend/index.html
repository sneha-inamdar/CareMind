CareMind Multimodal AI Engine Architecture

Temperature-Scaled Calibrated Fusion Model & Instability Index

1. Modality Encoders

Clinical vitals and continuous waveform signal streams (ECG, PPG, ABP) are standardized via StandardScaler and mapped to calibrated logit representations using LogisticRegression(C=1.0) models.

2. Temperature Logit Calibration

Raw decision function logits are calibrated with temperature dispersion ($T=1.75$) to expand the score dynamic range and prevent flat-line score saturation at 100.

3. Instability Index & Priority

Risk scores range continuously from 0 to 100 as a physiological instability index. Thresholds: <50 (Routine), 50–<75 (High Priority), ≥75 (Critical Priority).
